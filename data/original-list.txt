FOOD
BACCHANAL: a wine + cheese shop, with a HUGE backyard with live music. You buy wine, you buy cheese, then you pay the $3 for them to plate the cheese and sit in the backyard and just hang.  It gets PACKED on the weekends, so go early if you want to get a spot.  www.bacchanalwine.com (BYWATER) (take a cab… it’s at the end of the levy, and walking back at night can be sketch until you’re solidly back in the Marigny) 
Brother’s Food Mart: This is a gas station, but if you see one, get a piece of fried chicken there. http://brothersxpress.com/storelist.html (EVERYWHERE)
Cafe Du Monde: Beignets! So touristy but it's such a classic experience. My recommendation, get them to go and then go walk around the levy. www.cafedumonde.com (FRENCH QUARTER)
Cake Cafe: Really great brunch here too, casual, local place - shrimp/grits are perfect: http://nolacakes.com/ (MARIGNY)
Central Grocery: The home of the Muffuletta! It's amazing, also a lunch only place, get a half to go and walk around it's delicious: www.centralgrocerynola.com (FRENCH QUARTER)
Coops: Some touristy cajun food stuff but open late and pretty good! https://www.coopsplace.net/
Dooky Chase: This is where OBAMA gets fried chicken: www.dookychaserestaurant.com go for the all you can eat lunch buffet, it's $20 but it's worth it. (TREME)
Elizabeth’s: Great cajun brunch, get there early: www.elizabethsrestaurantnola.com (BYWATER)
Horns: Neighborhood Restaurant with Cajun Vibe, really great brunch: http://www.hornsnola.com/  (MARIGNY)
Johnny's Po-Boys: My favorite Po-Boys. Only open for lunch. SHRIMP all the way. www.yelp.com/biz/johnnys-po-boys-new-orleans (FRENCH QUARTER)
Jacques-Imo's - Really solid Cajun Food. The real deal! (UPTOWN). https://jacques-imos.com/
Mimi’s: If you find yourself out late, and need food, this is the spot. mimismarigny.com (MARIGNY)
Napoleon House. It's touristy, it's right in the french quarter, but the food is pretty good cajun food, they have great bloody mary's and a really solid pimm's cup.  www.napoleonhouse.com (FRENCH QUARTER)
Pizza Delicious: You wouldn't think to get pizza in New Orleans, but this is great for pizza outside of NY: www.pizzadelicious.com (BYWATER)
Port of Call - Burgers across the street from the Melrose Mansion www.portofcallnola.com
Satsuma’s: Really good brunch, chill, but low key atmosphere, nice to hang out outside. http://satsumacafe.com/ (BYWATER)
St. Roch Market: Kind of a “chelsea market” vibe, lots of different vendors, modern food, good cocktail place, good oysters and crawfish if in season! www.strochmarket.com (ST ROCH)
Turkey and the Wolf: Fried bologna sandwiches! And other good stuff. Good for brunch/lunch. www.turkeyandthewolf.com
Verti Mart - A bodega style po boy place (open late) in the Quarter. https://vertimartenola.com/
Willie Mae’s Scotch House: Pretty much right next door to Dooky Chase is another famous fried chicken  spot. www.williemaesnola.com (TREME)


LIVE MUSIC:
These are all places to see live music on Frenchman Street, they’re great, they all have full lineups every night, and just go and see what sounds good. Most of it’s going to be jazz, dixieland jazz, trad jazz, but it’s fun and lively and a lot of them you can listen to music on the street.
Spotted Cat
Three Muses
DBA
The Maison
Dragon’s Den
Also, go to ROYAL STREET in the French quarter in the day, there are going to be musicians busking on the street - Look for a band called TUBA SKINNY. They’re awesome.
Don’t skip Preservation Hall if you haven’t been
If you’re going uptown: Maple Leaf Bar https://www.mapleleafbar.com/

SIGHTS:
Ghost Tour, it’s silly, but it’s informative and fun. You can drink the whole way through: http://witchesbrewtours.com/portfolio/4-in-1-walking-tour/ (FRENCH QUARTER)
Frenchman Street Art Market: Cool art market!  http://frenchmenartmarket.com/ (MARIGNY)
French Market: It’s a street market - where you can buy many things with fleur de lis . http://www.frenchmarket.org/ (FRENCH QUARTER)
Euclid Records: Cool Record Shop in the Bywater (BYWATER)
Faulkner House Books: https://faulknerhousebooks.com/ former home of William Faulkner and nicely curated bookstore near Jackson Square (FRENCH QUARTER)

BARS: Most of the restaurants above have awesome bars, but here are a few worth noting:
Vaughns: Super Divy, cash only, but a great hang with live music. (BYWATER)
Lafitte’s: Oldest bar, used to be a blacksmith shop (FRENCH QUARTER)
Cane + Table: Fancy cocktails, really amazing curated cocktails, $$ but WORTH IT. (FRENCH QUARTER)
The Old Absinthe House: Touristy, but Absinthe, and historic! (FRENCH QUARTER)
The Country Club: Hipsters. A pool with a cabana bar, but can be a fun hang! www.thecountryclubneworleans.com (BYWATER)
BJ’s https://www.instagram.com/bjslounge/ good music (BYWATER)

UPTOWN: If you end up uptown, be sure to end up on Maple Street, then you can go to a really crazy bar in a garage in a residential neighborhood (Snake and Jake’s)
Jacques Imo’s Cafe A legit NOLA cajun/creole style restaurant.
Snake and Jake’s Christmas Club Lounge This is where Keith Richards hangs out in NOLA
Maple Leaf Bar https://www.mapleleafbar.com/
